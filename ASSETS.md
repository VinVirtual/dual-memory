# Third-party assets

Nothing third-party is bundled in this repository.

| Asset | Used for | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (ONNX export) | `OnnxEmbedder` and the "MiniLM" benchmark runs. Downloaded on first use into the Hugging Face cache, not committed. Pinned to Hub commit `1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41`; `onnx/model.onnx` SHA-256 `6fd5d72fe4589f189f8ebc006442dbb529bb7ce38f8082112682524616046452`, `tokenizer.json` SHA-256 `be50c3628f2bf5bb5e3a7f17b1f74611b2561a3a27eeab05e5aa30f411572037`. | DejaVu Sans (matplotlib's default font) | text in the benchmark chart PNGs | Bitstream Vera / public-domain changes | ships with matplotlib |

The benchmark data (`examples/benchmark/data.json`) is generated by
`dual_memory/bench/generate.py`. All people, names, places and preferences in
it are made up, picked from small fixed lists with a seeded random generator.

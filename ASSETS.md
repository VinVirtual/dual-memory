# Third-party assets

Nothing third-party is bundled in this repository.

| Asset | Used for | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (ONNX export) | `OnnxEmbedder` and the "MiniLM" benchmark run. Downloaded on first use into the Hugging Face cache, not committed. | Apache-2.0 | https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 |
| DejaVu Sans (matplotlib's default font) | text in the benchmark chart PNGs | Bitstream Vera / public-domain changes | ships with matplotlib |

The benchmark data (`examples/benchmark/data.json`) is generated by
`dual_memory/bench/generate.py`. All people, names, places and preferences in
it are made up, picked from small fixed lists with a seeded random generator.
